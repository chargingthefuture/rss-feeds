Feeds built from the public upload lists of YouTube channels, so that a feed reader can carry a channel's back catalog rather than the newest handful its own feed offers. A reader can only filter what has arrived, and a channel's own feed never delivers what came before; that is the only reason this exists.

A channel is listed here because one person reads it. A listing says nothing about the channel: it is not a recommendation, not an endorsement, not a claim of any kind about the people behind it, and not an association between them and Charging The Future. They have not been asked, and they have no connection to this project.

A cutoff date on a feed is that one reader's own decision about what they read, and nothing else. It is not a statement about what the channel published after that date.

Each entry carries the channel's own title, date and link, exactly as published and never altered; a title keeps its own spelling. No video is copied. An entry embeds the channel's own player and links to the video on YouTube.

These feeds are kept apart from the Charging The Future blog on purpose. The blog is that project's own writing. These are not, and nothing here is written in its voice.

If a channel is yours and you want it removed from here, open an issue on this repository. It is removed; no account on anything of ours is needed and no reason has to be given.
